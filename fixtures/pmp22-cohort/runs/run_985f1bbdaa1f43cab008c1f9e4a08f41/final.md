The audited data do not identify a state-matched, Schwann-intrinsic Nae1 effect on Pmp22 or antioxidant RNA at mouse P7. They do support a narrower result: normal Schwann-state differences include opposing antioxidant-gene responses, which a panel average conceals.

What survives the paired comparison

GSE137947 provides four mouse subjects with paired myelinating-enriched and not-myelinating-enriched Schwann gates. My retrospective analysis used subjects—not gates or cells—as biological units.

Pmp22, Nqo1 and Hmox1 directions agree across all four subjects, leave-one-subject analyses, and pseudocounts 0, 0.1 and 1. However, each has exact two-sided sign-flip p=0.125; the small sample does not support an assumption-light p<0.05 claim.

Thus, the near-null antioxidant average is not a uniform absence of response. Nqo1 rises while Hmox1 falls. These are normal enriched-gate associations, not pure-state measurements, Nae1 replication, or NRF2 mediation.

Where attribution remains blocked

  GSE241269: P7 whole-nerve genotype differences cannot locate the responding compartment or distinguish intrinsic expression from altered cell/RNA contributions.

  GSE138577: three P1 biological pools, with deliberate Schwann-cell depletion to approximately 30%. The three P60 pools are not additional neonatal replication. Captured-cell frequencies cannot supply tissue fractions.

  GSE177037: rat repair libraries are pooled; matching time labels and replicate suffixes do not establish donor pairing across purified cells and whole nerve. Their divergence remains a compartment warning, not neonatal mouse replication or proof of a macrophage response.

The precise inherited result that changed my analysis decision was failure of broad identity-marker calibration in the fixed P1 mixture model. I therefore did not fit new cell fractions or interpret atlas residuals as causal adjustment. Rejecting that reference model does not exclude actual composition changes or pathological P7 states.

I located a potentially discriminating dissociated-whole-nerve handling control in PMC9063194 Additional file 4, but actual downloads returned challenge HTML or failed. No supplementary values or matching assumptions were invented.

The missing decisive comparison is donor-labelled P7 Nae1-cKO/control lineage-resolved RNA, with both genotypes represented in common Schwann states and independent composition/RNA calibration. Lineage-total and state-conditioned responses should be reported separately.
