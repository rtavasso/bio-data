Human PMP22 data support cis-regulatory associations beyond clinical copy-number comparisons, but not yet a independently confirmed causal mechanism.

Strongest finding
  In GTEx v11 subcutaneous adipose (n=711), rs231016 C associates with lower use of a first-exon/common-exon junction: beta −0.1904, approximate 95% CI [−0.2570, −0.1238], q=0.00150.

  The exact junction matches exon1→exon2 boundaries in four GENCODE47 transcripts. The source seven-variant credible set remains ambiguous: highest PIP=0.410. This supports a first-exon-linked regulatory hypothesis—not promoter initiation, a percentage decrease in total RNA, or Schwann-cell specificity.

Independent tests
  The strong GTEx fibroblast eQTL at rs2531950 did not meet the frozen replication criterion in independent GENCORD fibroblasts: beta +0.0801, CI [−0.0981, +0.2582], p=0.380. Direction agrees, but replication is not established.

  Exact adipose-junction replication in TwinsUK was untestable from the retrieved export. Its reported nearby junction uses a different acceptor, four bases away; I did not substitute it or interpret missing rows as a negative.

Nerve and regulatory mapping
  Nerve-tibial eQTL q=0.0572 and sQTL q=0.365 do not pass 5% FDR. These are bounded threshold results, not evidence of absent regulation.

  Three selected fibroblast-associated variants overlap source-mapped human reporter fragments. Those overlaps generate hypotheses, not causal assignments or independent human functional validation.

Coverage and verification
  Audited 50 expression and 48 splicing contexts; enumerated all 8,593 distinct tested GENCORD PMP22 variants. Scientific assertions, prediction-link checks, ledger validation, completed notebook synchronization and all 16 shared artifact hashes passed.

  These are already-public associations. The contribution is the harmonized coverage audit, precise annotation, independent tests and preserved failures—not a claim of a newly discovered mechanism. Structural-variant linkage is not formally excluded.

Next discriminating question
  Does the exact rs231016–junction association replicate in independent adipose data, and does allele-matched nascent initiation change alongside junction usage? That separates initiation from processing/stability; Schwann-cell transfer requires a separate test.
