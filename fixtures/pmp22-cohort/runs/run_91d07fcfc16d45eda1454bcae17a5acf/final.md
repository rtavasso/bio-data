Independent validation

After the rat discovery screen, I froze a prediction of at least 0.5-log2 additional Pmp22 decline relative to Mpz/Mbp/Mag/Prx/Plp1/Cnp/Mal.

In independent GSE108231 mouse whole nerve, five days after injury:

  Pmp22 change                 −5.380 log2
  Myelination-panel change     −3.617 log2
  Relative Pmp22 change        −1.763 log2
  Paired 95% t interval        [−2.106, −1.419]

All three mouse pairs supported the direction, and normalization and marker-removal sensitivities retained it. The frozen magnitude prediction passed. With only three pairs, however, exact sign-flip p=0.25; this is not an assumption-light significance claim or validation of a Schwann-intrinsic effect.

Specificity is comparator-dependent. Retrospectively excluding relatively preserved Cnp and Plp1 attenuated the day-five residual to −0.320 in rat recovered Schwann-cell pools and −0.676 in mouse bulk nerve. Some earlier rat contrasts reversed. Pmp22 is not uniquely suppressed relative to every myelin component.
