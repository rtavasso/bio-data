# PMP22 RNA fate: human UTR support, fibroblast RIDD, and unidentified Schwann rates

Main conclusions:

1. miR-29a has seed-dependent PMP22 repression in newborn-rat Schwann cultures, with anti-miR response and transfected Ago2 association (PMID19170179, Figures5-7). This is not endogenous AGO-loading quantification or a decay/translation decomposition.

2. Newly incorporated human evidence strengthens physiological UTR relevance: a 650-bp deletion pedigree and recreated deletion reporters resistant to miR-29a (PMID37337674/PMC10545524, Figure4). Two proband biopsies are one donor; the deletion spans other predicted miRNA sites; reporters are RT4/Oli-neu, not primary human Schwann cells. This is recovered known literature, not discovery. Human fibroblast RNA48h/protein72h in PMC6920087 remains an unmatched endpoint pair.

3. PMC2728407 TableI reports Pmp22 destabilization under DTT in reconstituted mouse fibroblasts, referring to the actinomycin-D assay. Published log2(DTT/untreated) means are -0.88 in hIre1R and -0.07 in Ire1-null; their retrospectively calculated difference is -0.81, with no new interval or significance claim. The arrays used 6h stress; separate qPCR used 5h. This supports context-specific IRE1-linked decay, not Schwann validation. A PMP22-specific RNase-mutant decay interaction or cleavage site remains unverified.

4. G3BP1 depletion raises PMP22 RNA in MCF-7 without a significant reported half-life change (PMC3866477, Table1). That negative is not equivalence. Acute versus developmental Dicer perturbations also differ: long-term processing loss changes differentiation. Upstream miRNA abundance, AGO loading and cell state must be separated. IGF2BP3/EDC3 stress-dependent regulation and circRNA competition are explicitly hypotheses for PMP22, not validated route assignments.

Proposed falsifier: state-matched Schwann cells with nascent output and multi-timepoint labelled-RNA loss, crossed with miR29-site/anti-miR and ERN1-WT/RNase-inactive controls. Increased decay must produce faster disappearance rescued by the appropriate route-specific intervention. Adequately precise equivalence of survival despite RNA loss, together with reduced nascent output, would reject increased decay as sufficient in that context. Nonsignificance alone would not.
