## Answer

Human dosage relevance is stronger than a rodent-transfer-only account. Within-background human interventions support changes in total mature PMP22 RNA, total protein and structural myelin-associated phenotypes. However, none of the nine audited contexts establishes a donor-general, maturation-independent genomic-copy-number to functional-PMP22 response curve. Approximately proportional output, compensation, altered protein handling and cell-state/composition effects remain distinguishable hypotheses, not resolved mechanisms.

## What changes the human evidence assessment

1. Repeated CS67 papers are not independent CMT1A donor replication. PMC6612317 uses TALEN start-codon inactivation of one PMP22 allele; PMC10316758 organoids and PMC11370802 lipid work reuse that donor lineage. The later n1 versus original n4 clone labels remain unresolved, not silently equated. The lipid study's ddPCR probe targets the corrected region: 1.89 versus 2.98 detectable copies does not independently show whole-duplication excision.

2. Selection matters. PMC6612317's multi-donor arm includes four patients in two parent-child pairs, and selects subsequent preparations for at least 1.5-fold PMP22 elevation. Its transcriptome/secretome contrasts cannot estimate the frequency of neutral or buffered RNA output in unselected CMT1A cells.

3. Human structural rescue has support, but not every endpoint moves together. PMC10316758 reports organoid g-ratio/periodicity improvement; neuron counts and two differentiation batches are not independent donors. Small-axon hypermyelination and shRNA-1 lowering RNA without restoring p-Akt/Akt are retained. p-Akt blots are not quantitative PMP22 protein. Mixed organoids do not isolate a Schwann-cell-autonomous effect.

4. Neutral and contrary tissue evidence is retained. PMC7050273's skin RNA-seq pilot reports approximately proportional elevation (~1.55-fold); normalized Nanostring produces a different magnitude and variable CMT1A expression, including first-exon-resolved mature RNA. That is not nascent promoter output. PMC2724915 reports roughly half-normal HNPP myelin protein and variable CMT1A levels, with no detected severity correlation. Its RNA and protein came from different subjects, so a delivery-efficiency ratio is not calculable.

5. GSE7423 is not a replicated human dosage test. Twelve arrays map to seven donor labels; only BAR (GSM178756/757) has duplication. JIM (GSM178758/759) has Leu16Pro. Normal ETT appears twice; the shared reference pool is not a new donor. Plantar-versus-sural source and mitogen-expanded state confound the contrast. VALUE is log10 Cy3/Cy5; no first-exon validation or protein endpoint. The acquired quick-view measurements are incomplete and were used only for metadata. BONb's conflicting replicate label is preserved.
