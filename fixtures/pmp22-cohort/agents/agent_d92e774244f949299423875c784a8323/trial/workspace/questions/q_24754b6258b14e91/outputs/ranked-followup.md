# Ranked mechanistic follow-up and uncertainty map

Question q_24754b6258b14e91. These are researcher judgments informed by the executed tables, not additional measured results.

1. Adipose first-exon-linked splice association (leading mechanistic candidate; association supported, independent replication unresolved).
   GTEx v11 rs231016 C associates with lower normalized use of the exact minus-strand 15260761:15265154 junction. Four GENCODE47 transcripts use it as exon1→exon2. The source seven-variant credible set spans GRCh38 chr17:15251649–15264358; largest PIP is only0.410. None of the selected adipose sQTL variants overlaps the thirteen mapped reporter-fragment cores. Do not infer which first-exon promoter initiates less, or elevate the lead SNP to a causal nucleotide.
   Competing explanations: altered first-exon initiation, donor/acceptor choice, stability of junction-containing RNA, genotype-associated tissue composition, or a correlated variant. Test the same exact junction and allele first in an independent adipose summary resource with documented all-feature coverage. For mechanism, measure both first-exon/common-exon junctions, total RNA and capped nascent initiation in a matched genotype/edited-haplotype context; include matched intron-processing and cell-composition controls. The two highest-PIP variants and full seven-variant set remain competing perturbation targets rather than one winner. Test Schwann-cell transfer separately; purified adipocytes are also not Schwann cells.

2. Fibroblast cis-expression region (strong GTEx discovery; failed preselected independent transfer).
   The rs2531950 exact-allele test did not replicate at the sealed significance threshold in GENCORD; wide uncertainty does not refute the GTEx effect or establish age/context interaction. All8593 independent-cohort variants were enumerated;110 of113 selected GTEx fibroblast variants are present. Three selected GTEx variants overlap previously assayed human-sequence reporters: chr17_15247170_C_CT_b38 in the larger +11kb/Hsp68 fragment, chr17_15254536_A_G_b38 in +5kb, and chr17_15269904_C_A_b38 in −7kb. The common insertion representation's REF anchor lies in the reporter core; this is not a claim about sequence-function change. Neither the source fibroblast lead nor overlap alone identifies one causal element. No GTEx v11 PMP22 eQTL credible-set row was present in the source summary; why it is absent was not inferred.
   Discriminating measurement: independent adult fibroblast cohort with same variant/allele and well-covered PMP22, then phased perturbations with endogenous RNA readout. Include different ages/culture protocols without mistaking a p-value difference for heterogeneity. Reporter tests of human DNA in rodent cells are prior cis evidence, not independent human variant validation.

3. Nerve-tibial transfer (bounded negative at chosen multiple-testing cutoff; not equivalence).
   eQTL q=.0571736 and sQTL q=.365171 do not meet source5%FDR. The gene and six splice features were tested in667 bulk-nerve samples with source-reported9060 variants, but exact all-tested SNP statistics were not retrieved. Tissue mixture, power, normalization and release/annotation changes can explain the threshold outcome. Obtain harmonized all-pairs statistics and cell-type-resolved data before asserting nerve specificity or absence. Do not promote v8/v11 discordance to replication or a biological reversal.

4. Ovary splice association (source-supported, lower priority for PMP22-control transfer).
   Distinct internal-junction signal has source q=.0249208, a one-variant source credible set/PIP.998929, and only12 minor-allele carriers. This is not proof of causality or a nerve mechanism. An independent same-junction cohort and rare-allele QC are more informative than confidence from the high PIP alone.

Novelty and source boundaries
   GTEx associations and SuSiE rows are already-public results. This audit adds explicit tested-feature/variant coverage, two frozen validation attempts, source-compatible human mapping and a falsifiable first-exon-processing hypothesis—not a new causal mechanism. Cis reporters and the human3'UTR deletion are established prior mechanisms. Focused literature search of PMP22 and eQTL/sQTL returned57 records; exact rs231016 query returned no indexed records in that search, not proof of novelty. The current RBP peer post_39c7e08ff5fa43498fe97be20590edf5 has binding but no multiplicity-supported total-RNA response hit and already recognizes a human QKI isoform paper; no QKI novelty claim is made here.
   RNA-ends peer post_5a072afa4a8641a5aa0e37f97eb4537c independently reviewed the current junction interpretation as first-exon/common-exon linkage. Its end-site/FLAIR evidence is an annotation/context critique, not QTL replication. Exact650-bp deletion endpoints and genotype-to-cleavage coupling are not established. No natural APA inference comes from this sQTL.
   SNP/indel association is evidence of regulatory variation beyond merely comparing clinical copy-number classes, but these public summaries do not condition on measured PMP22 structural copy number. Linkage to a structural variant is not formally excluded. No gene-expression result measures initiation, surface/myelin protein, protein turnover or a copy-number dose curve.
