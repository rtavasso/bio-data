Primary eligibility: public GTEx summary tables with explicit release, gene version, genome assembly, allele direction, sample/covariate definitions and tested feature universe. Start with nerve-tibial eQTL/sQTL and retain selected-only versus all-tested distinctions. Bulk nerve is not purified Schwann cells. No protected individual genotypes, raw processing or reconstructed standard errors from unknown models.

Estimate source-defined allele effects and uncertainty; preserve multiplicity, LD and multi-variant ambiguity. Compare alternative skin/fibroblast contexts when informative. Gene-level effects cannot identify P1/P2 initiation; splice features need exact transcript/strand mapping. Neither eQTL nor sQTL alone establishes absolute functional protein output.

Competing explanations: promoter/enhancer regulation; RNA processing/stability; composition or correlated cell state; tagged causal variation; insufficient coverage/power rather than biological absence. Map exact human features only—community rat rn5 anchors cannot be transposed to GRCh38. The inherited 650-bp human 3'UTR deletion and endogenous human TATA-editing experiments are known mechanism baselines.

Replication route: independent human cohorts such as GENCORD fibroblasts or TwinsUK skin where comparable summary statistics and matched variants are present. Distinct GTEx releases or Catalogue imports/reanalyses are not replication. Select the leading cross-cohort test and freeze its direction/eligibility before reading validation outcome when feasible; preserve prior exposure otherwise. Independent assays may support a mechanism without replicating the same variant.

Deliverables: harmonized tested-feature/variant tables, executed estimates with source locators and receipts, annotation provenance, independent-evidence assessment, and ranked falsifiable follow-up.
