The key distinction is RNA discordance versus a protein-abundance-only explanation. Pmp22-minus-Egr2/Sox10 can quantify unequal RNA responses, but it cannot exclude nonlinear regulation by the remaining protein, altered nuclear availability, cofactors, or developmental composition.

Matched evidence I would prioritize

1. Quantitative total and nuclear EGR2 and SOX10 in identified Schwann cells, at the same genotype, age and harvest as the output assay. Use prospective preservation bounds rather than unchanged-looking bands, percent-positive cells, or nonsignificance. Prefer matched aliquots from documented biological units; pooled-library labels cannot create donor or litter independence.

2. Calibrated EGR2/SOX10 occupancy at independently specified Pmp22 regulatory elements, alongside TEAD1 and appropriate negative/reference sites. Measure accessibility in parallel; do not require or assume it is unchanged. Stable nuclear regulator amounts with reduced binding would argue against abundance alone, but would not distinguish changed accessibility from recruitment/cofactor failure without the accompanying measurements.

3. Endogenous nascent Pmp22 output in the same condition/time, with P1/P2 resolution if initiation is the claim. Stable abundance and occupancy with reduced nascent output would favor a defect after binding or in a cooperating pathway, rather than establish EGR2 independence. Total RNA or mature first-exon RT-qPCR cannot substitute for promoter-specific initiation.

4. For a stronger abundance-only test, restore BOTH regulators to a physiological nuclear range under the upstream perturbation and verify productive engagement at independent positive-control loci as well as occupancy at Pmp22. If Pmp22 nascent output remains deficient before state divergence, abundance restoration is insufficient. If it rescues, sufficiency is supported, not exclusive mediation. A proposed endogenous TEAD-site edit by perturbation interaction with precise repair rescue would then test locus-specific TEAD necessity; protect against neighboring-motif disruption and baseline-floor artifacts. This is a proposed discriminator, not an existing result.

Record proliferation, viability, maturation and Schwann-cell fractions separately. Myelin7 remains a fixed output comparator, not a direct activity assay or causal adjustment variable.
