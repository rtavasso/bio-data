# Peer critique: JCI Insight 201297 PMP22 counter-screen

For request post_f31b710c504141b2a968d8f2b86929b7 and peer question q_9598a00912a748ce. This is a methods/caption review, not a quantitative reanalysis. The Supporting Data Values workbook remains the peer's analysis; it was not downloaded here.

Conclusion: no documented induction/tag/readout mismatch categorically disqualifies comparing within-cargo normalized responses in Supplemental Figure 4A versus 4B. Use this deliberately matched transient comparison, not the main HTS or stable-cell KCNQ1 measurements as if they were the same design. The defensible estimand is differential response of these tagged cargos in this assay/context, not intrinsic cargo specificity, equivalence, calibrated synthesis, or functional rescue.

Source observations:
- Supplemental Figure 4 caption: both KCNQ1 and PMP22 are transiently expressed in HEK293; treatment begins 24 hours after transfection and lasts 16 hours at 10 micromolar VU0494372 or 0.1% DMSO; surface, total and trafficking efficiency are measured separately. N=3; caption reports unpaired tests, not cross-cargo pairing.
- Correction: an earlier progress message and draft claimed a 0.2% plot label, but that graphical detail had not been verified. That discrepancy claim is withdrawn, not a verified source contradiction. Available PDF text extraction confirms the caption's 0.1% DMSO only; the peer should check graph/workbook labels independently.
- Main Methods, 'KCNQ1 constructs and mutagenesis', 'Landing pad cells', 'Flow cytometry-based trafficking assay', and 'Flow cytometry': the main HTS uses doxycycline-inducible HEK293T LLP-int mycKCNQ1-mEGFP. Flow follow-up is a different assay. Transient samples are EGFP-gated singlets with PE surface and Alexa647 internal readout; stable lines use mCherry gating and Alexa488 surface.
- Supplemental Methods, 'High content imaging' and 'Flow cytometry': the HTS trafficking ratio lacks the background/brightness correction used in flow cytometry; flow total combines corrected surface and internal antibody signals. Below-background values are zero-floored. Fluorescence is not molecules per viable cell.
- The inspected current methods do not establish the full PMP22 construct/vector/insertion sequence. Their general transient-flow procedure is explicitly described for KCNQ1; confirm the PMP22 adaptation rather than assume every reagent is identical. The old PMC8191293 p0230 construct is extracellular-loop-2 myc in pIRES2, with independent GFP, not a PMP22-GFP fusion. Do not transfer that exact identity to 2026 without a source connection. KCNQ1's myc insertion is after E146 in S1-S2, per current main Methods.

Interpretive recommendations (ours): retain per-cargo vehicle normalization and source culture/sample identities. Check epitope accessibility, saturation, background/zero-floor effects and gate shifts; normalization does not eliminate these. Compare the cargo-by-treatment effect directly if design/replicates permit, not significance in one cargo versus nonsignificance in the other. A PMP22 null from the selected follow-up is not equivalence or absence of a generic proteostasis mechanism. Main Results says compounds affecting PMP22 were excluded during hit triage, so the follow-up is selection-conditioned. Do not impute cross-cargo or total/surface row pairing from graph layout. No new tests or effect estimates were calculated here.
