## Independent measurement checks

The parent audit used separate scripts in `workspaces/deep-investigation-source-audit/`. These checks were not supplied to the subject or the independent reviewer. They reproduce extraction and arithmetic; they do not establish causal mechanisms.

- **Egr2-AS RNA:** direct source-TSV recomputation reproduced 17,949 numeric rows, 1,623 all-zero rows, the embedded-header exclusion, size factors, sample-structure variance and descriptive AS/control ratios: Pmp22 **0.885**, Egr2 **1.258**, Jun **0.820**. Those directions persist across the agent's normalization checks. The deposited RNA and protein experiments are not demonstrated to be matched cultures or endpoints.
- **PMP22 protein interactions:** direct workbook inspection reproduced 56 WT, 485 N41Q and 129 L16P selected entries, 515 distinct proteins and 40 entries shared across all three lists. All 15 reported descriptive comparisons reproduced. These are enrichment-over-mock summaries, not fresh differential tests, direct-binding proof or a complete measured universe. The primary paper itself identifies variable detection power across batches.
- **Quantitative accessibility:** a separately written decoder reproduced all ten PMP22-region observed-base means and all five common-base comparisons from the two deposited TDF files. The chromosome contained zero-width records; they contribute no bases to interval means. This checks two implementations against the same preserved format specification, not against an established independent reader. There is one library per genotype and different DRG/tumor annotations; these are descriptive measurements, not a clean causal RUNX estimate.
- **Human expression:** direct matrix parsing reproduced the seven donor labels and the two probe calculations. One duplication donor and one different L16P donor cannot supply independent duplication replicates. The two PMP22 probes give markedly different magnitudes; the source's log10 scale, pooled reference and culture context matter.
- **New RUNX bulk RNA:** direct reading of the six archived count members reproduced 24,062 gene rows and the fresh normalized ratios: Pmp22 **2.308**, Mbp **2.475**, Mpz **2.820**. Four sample titles disagree with corresponding file headers, with swaps within groups. Group means survive those swaps; animal-level pairing and promoter attribution do not follow. Tissue/state confounding remains.

The useful discoveries include design and interpretation problems, not only candidate regulators: a library-specific hemoglobin signal, mismatched RNA/protein endpoints, genotype pooling under a shared disease label, source sample-label swaps, selected-list omissions, and the difference between a protein's association with PMP22 and its functional effect on trafficking.
